**Not a signal service.** Forward-looking entries — the "anticipated moves" board, the
entry watch, the `will` entries in the reasoning log — describe what an automated agent
intends to do under stated conditions. They are published *before* the fact so the
reasoning can be graded honestly *after* the fact. That is the entire purpose. They are
not published so anyone can trade alongside them, and doing so is a bad idea:

- The account is small enough to be irrelevant. Position sizing here means nothing at any other size.
- The board shows the state as of the last committed run. It is not live. By the time you read a level, it may have been hit, missed, or cancelled.
- The sample is tiny. Rates computed from a handful of trades are noise wearing a percentage sign.
